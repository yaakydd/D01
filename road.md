# Kloud Kart: 12-Month Data Strategy Roadmap

| Quarter               | Main priority                                         | Work to be completed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Verifiable business outcome (who, what changes, how it is checked)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Q1: Stop the harm** | Customer data, website protection, agreed definitions | Management names an owner for the Data Protection Commission (DPC) complaint and the customer-data inventory. Investigate and respond. List every place customer data sits, with an owner for each. Hire the Data Engineer. Provision and test the read replica. Move the Monday extract onto it. Nana, Finance and a management sponsor agree one-page definitions of revenue, completed orders and cancellations. | **1.** The DPC response is submitted within the 90-day deadline, with a written list of every place the customer's data was held and what was done at each. **2.** By the end of Q1, the Monday extract runs from the replica outside busy periods. Site response time during the export matches an ordinary Monday hour, and the developer shows Nana and the board the before-and-after timings. **3.** By the end of Q1, Nana and Finance have both signed one page defining revenue, completed orders and cancellations, which either can read without help. |
| **Q2: Reconcile**     | One set of figures                                    | Hire the Analytics Engineer at the start of Q2. Build the modest warehouse from the replica using only the permitted data. Write the agreed definitions into it. Publish scheduled daily reports. **Checkpoint:** confirm the warehouse is still needed before spending more on it.                                                                                                                                 | **1.** By the end of Q2, for one full month, Nana's Monday figures and Finance's month-end revenue match, and every earlier difference has a written explanation. **2.** Nana opens one shared report herself and no longer rebuilds Monday numbers from CSV exports.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Q3: Control**       | Trust in the figures and customer-data controls       | Add checks for missing and duplicated data. Show each report's last update time. Alert a named owner when an update or check fails. Test a customer deletion request across every listed location. Review who can see customer data.                                                                                                                                                                                | **1.** Every Monday report shows when it was last updated and whether its checks passed. **2.** When a figure fails a check, the named owner is alerted before Nana notices, and the board can see a list of problems found and who fixed them. **3.** A test deletion request is completed across every listed location in the time management sets, with the evidence kept.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Q4: Review**        | Cost, reliability and what comes next                 | Review reporting reliability and monthly costs against the approved budget. Assess Nigeria's reporting needs. Name any decision that needs data fresher than a day, with its cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | **1.** The board receives a one-page cost report showing actual spend against the approved budget for every month of the year. **2.** The board receives a one-page paper stating whether daily reporting is still enough for the Nigeria pilot, naming any decision that needs faster data and what it would cost.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

_Assumptions: the Data Engineer is hired early in Q1 and the Analytics Engineer at the start of Q2; the existing developer can begin the replica if hiring slips (to be confirmed); Nana and Finance are available to agree the definitions. The DPC start date and all costs are unknown and must be confirmed._
