# Kloud Kart: 12-Month Data Strategy Roadmap

| Quarter | Main priority | Work to be completed | Verifiable business outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1: Address immediate risks** | Customer-data protection and website performance | Investigate the Data Protection Commission complaint and submit a response within the 90-day deadline. Identify known locations of customer information, assign departmental owners and establish procedures for access and deletion. Hire the Data Engineer and establish and test the MySQL read replica using the approved second server. Move reporting exports away from the live database. | The DPC response has been submitted within the deadline, with supporting evidence. Known customer-data locations have assigned owners. Reporting exports no longer run against the live database, reducing the risk of reporting work slowing down the website. |
| **Q2: Establish reliable reporting** | Consistent business figures | Hire the Analytics Engineer. Set up the modest cloud data warehouse using permitted data from the read replica. Agree with Nana and Finance on how key figures, especially revenue, are calculated. Replace separate manual spreadsheet calculations with scheduled reports. | Nana and Finance receive the same Monday figures using agreed calculations and reporting periods. Nana no longer needs to rebuild reports from large CSV exports. |
| **Q3: Improve confidence in the data** | Reporting quality and customer-data controls | Introduce checks for missing, duplicated or inconsistent reporting data. Monitor scheduled reports so failures are identified and corrected. Review access to customer information and test whether deletion procedures cover the known data locations. | Management can identify reporting errors and failed updates. Reports show when they were last updated, and identified problems can be followed up. Management has evidence that customer-data access and deletion procedures are working. |
| **Q4: Prepare for future growth** | Review and plan the next investment | Review reporting reliability and operating costs. Assess whether the current arrangement can support Nigeria's orders, inventory and reporting requirements. Consider forecasting or more frequent reporting only where a clear business need has been identified. | Management has evidence that reporting is reliable, understands the cost of maintaining the system and can make an informed decision about future data investments. |
